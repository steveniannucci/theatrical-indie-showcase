THEATRICAL INDIE SHOWCASE

Anora

Genre: Romance

Release Date (US): October 18th, 2024

Distributer: NEON

Budget: $6M

Box Office: $57.4M

    Notable Achievements:
  • Won five Oscars at the 97th Academy Awards, three of which were for Best Picture, Best Director, and Best Original Screenplay. Mikey Madison, the leading actress also won the Oscar for Best Actress and the director, Sean Baker, won the Oscar for Best Film Editing.
  • Won the Palme d'Or, the highest prize awarded for best feature film at the Cannes Film Festival.
  • Won Best Picture at the Critics' Choice Awards.

Description: A young stripper from Brooklyn meets and impulsively marries the son of a Russian oligarch. Once the news reaches Russia, her fairy tale is threatened as his parents set out for New York to get the marriage annulled.
