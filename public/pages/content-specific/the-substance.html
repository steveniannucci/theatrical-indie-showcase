THEATRICAL INDIE SHOWCASE

The Substance

Genre: Horror, Science Fiction

Release Date (US): September 20th, 2024

Distributer: MUBI

Budget: $18M

Box Office: $77.3M

    Notable Achievements:
  • Became MUBI's highest-grossing film after they've successfully acquired the worldwide distribution rights from Universal Pictures. The reason Universal stepped away from "The Substance" project was due to concerns with its extreme body horror content.
  • Won Best Screenplay at the Cannes Film Festival and was nominated for the Palme d'Or, the highest prize awarded for best feature film.

Description: A fading celebrity takes a black-market drug: a cell-replicating substance that helps her create a younger, better version of herself.
