THEATRICAL INDIE SHOWCASE

Flow

Genre: Adventure, Fantasy

Release Date (US): November 22, 2024

Distributers: Janus Films, Sideshow

Budget: $3.4M

Box Office: almost $31M

    Notable Achievements:
  • First non-dialogue film to win Best Animated Feature.
  • Awarded "Best European Feature-Length Animated Film" from the European Film Academy.
  • Won a Golden Globe and an Oscar for Best Animated Feature in 2025.

Description: A wondrous journey, through realms natural and mystical, Flow follows a courageous cat after his home is devastated by a great flood. Teaming up with a capybara, a lemur, a bird, and a dog to navigate a boat in search of dry land, they must rely on trust, courage, and wits to survive the perils of a newly aquatic planet.
